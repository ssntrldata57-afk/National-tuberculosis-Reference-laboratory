About the National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory

The National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory (NTRL) is a specialized laboratory supporting tuberculosis diagnosis, laboratory quality assurance, surveillance, research, training and capacity building in South Sudan.

NTRL works closely with the Ministry of Health, National Tuberculosis Program, public health laboratories, health facilities and development partners to strengthen tuberculosis laboratory services across the country.

MISSION

To provide reliable, timely and quality-assured tuberculosis laboratory services.

VISION

A strong, quality-driven TB laboratory system supporting a healthier South Sudan.

DIRECTOR'S MESSAGE

Welcome to the National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the official website of the National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory.

NTRL is committed to strengthening tuberculosis laboratory services through quality diagnostics, laboratory quality assurance, surveillance, research, training and technical support.

Through collaboration with the Ministry of Health, National Tuberculosis Program, health facilities, laboratory networks and development partners, we continue working toward a stronger and more responsive tuberculosis laboratory system in South Sudan.

We remain committed to continuous quality improvement, professional development and the provision of accurate laboratory information to support public health decision-making.
