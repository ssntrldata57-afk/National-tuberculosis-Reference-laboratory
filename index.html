About NTRL

The National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory (NTRL) is a specialized laboratory supporting tuberculosis diagnosis, quality assurance, surveillance, research and capacity building in South Sudan.

NTRL works closely with the Ministry of Health, National Tuberculosis Program, public health laboratories, health facilities and development partners to strengthen TB laboratory services across the country.

Laboratory Services

TB Diagnostic Services

Molecular and laboratory testing to support accurate and timely tuberculosis diagnosis.

Quality Assurance

External Quality Assessment (EQA), microscopy rechecking and laboratory quality improvement.

Culture & Drug Susceptibility Testing

Laboratory services supporting tuberculosis culture and drug resistance testing.

Training & Mentorship

Capacity building, training and mentorship for TB laboratory personnel.

TB Testing

NTRL supports tuberculosis testing through modern laboratory diagnostic technologies and quality-assured testing approaches.

  • GeneXpert MTB/RIF testing
  • Microscopy services
  • TB culture
  • Drug susceptibility testing
  • TB laboratory quality assurance
  • Sample referral and laboratory surveillance

Data & Reporting

Laboratory data management and reporting are essential for monitoring TB diagnostic services and supporting evidence- based public health decision-making.

Data Management

Collection, validation, analysis and management of laboratory and TB diagnostic data.

Electronic Systems

Supporting electronic laboratory information systems and integration with national health information platforms.

Reporting

Routine laboratory reporting, monitoring indicators and supporting program performance reviews.
