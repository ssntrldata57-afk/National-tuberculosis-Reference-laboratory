ABOUT NTRL

National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory

The National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory (NTRL) provides specialized laboratory support for tuberculosis diagnosis, monitoring, quality assurance and laboratory capacity development.

The laboratory supports the national tuberculosis response by promoting accurate, reliable and timely laboratory testing and strengthening laboratory systems.

Our work contributes to improved diagnosis, surveillance, quality management and technical capacity within tuberculosis laboratory services.

Our Role

  • TB laboratory diagnosis
  • Quality assurance
  • Laboratory supervision
  • Technical support
  • Training and capacity building
  • Laboratory surveillance support
