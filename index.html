OUR MISSION

Supporting Quality Tuberculosis Diagnosis

The National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory (NTRL) serves as a specialized laboratory facility supporting the national tuberculosis response in South Sudan.

Through quality-assured diagnostic services, laboratory surveillance, technical support, quality management and capacity building, NTRL contributes to accurate and timely tuberculosis diagnosis and improved patient care.

ABOUT NTRL

National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory

A national centre for specialized tuberculosis laboratory services, quality assurance and technical support.

Our Role

The National Tuberculosis Reference Laboratory provides specialized laboratory support for tuberculosis diagnosis, monitoring, quality assurance and laboratory capacity development.

NTRL works with the national tuberculosis programme, public health laboratories and other relevant partners to strengthen tuberculosis laboratory services and improve the quality and availability of diagnostic testing.

Our work supports the national TB response by promoting accurate, reliable and timely laboratory testing while strengthening laboratory systems, quality management and technical capacity.
